# Start: Findings to management actions

Use this skill to make evaluation findings actionable through a traceable response and action matrix. It separates evidence, management decisions, and proposed actions. Suggested owners and dates are not commitments.

**Intake:** attach findings and relevant responses, or name a folder. For a folder, ask the assistant to list files before selecting them. It must state the intake mode.

**Starter:**

> Use findings-to-actions on the attached files [or folder: path]. Create a source-linked management response and action matrix. Separate finding, evidence, management decision, and proposed action. Label any suggested owner or deadline as a proposal, and do not present it as a commitment.

Examples are fictional illustrations, not live test evidence.
